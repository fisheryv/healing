Core Features
Procedural Artwork
5 mathematical equations including Lissajous curves, with audio injecting real-time parameters. Every session generates a unique painting — the reveal when you flip your phone is ADHD's strongest intrinsic motivator.
3-Track Mixer
Main music + white noise (11 options) + binaural beat (4 bands), real-time mix preview, presets can be named and saved. Alpha wave + rain + Lo-fi layered immersion.
Gallery & Relics
Completed works and interrupted "Relics" archived with literary excerpts and session info. Share artwork + quote as a long image to social platforms.
Activity Heatmap
52-week focus history visualized GitHub-style, color depth mapped to daily session count. Tracks streaks, total time, and gallery count for positive reinforcement.
